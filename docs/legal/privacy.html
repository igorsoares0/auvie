Auvie

Privacy

Effective [date of publication]

Your photos and films never leave your phone. Auvie edits them on your device and saves the results to your gallery. There is no Auvie account, and we have no servers that receive your media, your edits or anything you type.

What stays on your phone

  • Your edits (adjustments, crops, text, drawings, trims), their thumbnails, your favorites and your settings. They live in the app's private storage and are deleted when you delete an edit or uninstall the app.
  • Exported photos and videos, saved to your gallery (Pictures/Auvie and Movies/Auvie). The app also keeps a temporary copy for sharing, which it removes after a day or when you clear it in Settings.

Access Auvie asks for

  • Photos and videos: only the ones you choose in the Android photo picker. Auvie can't browse your gallery.
  • Notifications: to show the progress of a video export. Saying no doesn't stop exports.
  • Internet: only for buying and restoring Auvie Pro.

Buying Auvie Pro

Payments are handled by Google Play; Auvie never sees your card. To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for this installation (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription history from Google Play, and basic technical information such as the app version, your device's operating system and country. RevenueCat's own policy: revenuecat.com/privacy. Google's: policies.google.com/privacy.

Crashes

If the app crashes or freezes and you allow Android to share usage and diagnostics, Google Play may send us an anonymous crash report (the error and the device model) through the Play Console. It never contains your photos, videos or edits.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no selling or sharing of data.
  • No analytics or tracking in this version. If that changes, this page will say so before the app does.

Children

Auvie is not directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can delete any edit from the Home screen (hold it), clear cached files in Settings, and remove everything by uninstalling the app. To manage or cancel Pro, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. For questions about purchase data held by RevenueCat, write to us and we'll handle the request with them.
